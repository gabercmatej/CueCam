Your webcam, reacting to your hands and voice.

Give a thumbs up, make a fist, pinch to draw in the air, or say “boom”. Hand tracking, segmentation and speech recognition all run on your machine, in this tab.

Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. First load ≈ 30 MB (models and effects, cached afterwards); voice adds 41 MB, only if you turn it on.

Voice & clap Say “boom”, “let’s go”, “I love you” or “bye”, and clap. Needs the microphone and a 41 MB speech model.

Private by design. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your camera and microphone never leave this tab: nothing is uploaded, recorded or stored. The page only downloads the models and effects.

What to try
  • 👍 👎 ✌️ ☝️ 👉 one hand: a reaction in the corner
  • ✊ fist: confetti from that hand (both hands work)
  • 🤏 pinch and move: draw light in the air
  • 🤘 rock: guitar riff · 🙏 pray · 🙌 both hands up · 🫶 finger heart with two hands
  • ✋ open palm + say “fire”: a fireball on your palm
  • “boom”: an explosion behind you · “bye” at the end of a sentence: curtains

Headphones help once voice is on: effect sounds from speakers can reach the mic.

Controls appear here once the camera is running: arm and disarm (K), what was recognized, and a Test button for every effect.
